Description

Elewar Gaming is an independent game development entity focused on creating engaging action games and interactive experiences for PC platforms.

Founded in 2023 by Alexander Krasnobaev, Elewar Gaming released its debut title 'Elewar: Fused Survivors' on Steam in 2024. The studio focuses on core gameplay mechanics, action systems, and PC game development.
